# LabelLoom tutorial

[한국어](../README.md)

LabelLoom designs labels, name badges and barcodes in the browser and prints them in bulk from a spreadsheet.
There is nothing to install: open [https://isaac-kwon.github.io/LabelLoom/](https://isaac-kwon.github.io/LabelLoom/) and start.

## Follow along in the app

The four lessons below are built into the app as well.

- **On your first visit**, the start screen opens on the **Tutorial** tab.
- Later, use **File › Tutorial · Help** or press **F1**.
- **Start the walkthrough** opens an example project and points at each button in turn. Do the step and the tour moves on, or press **Skip**.
- Opening an example keeps your current work under **Recently saved** on the start screen.

The app is also available in Korean, Italian and French (**File** menu, language list). These pages use the English names of buttons and menus.

| | Lesson | Example | What you learn |

New to LabelLoom? Go through them in order. If you just need badges printed today, lessons 2 and 4 are enough.

## Good to know

- **Your data never leaves your browser.** There is no server; your work is saved in the browser automatically. To carry on on another computer, use **File › Save** to make a `.labelloom.json` file.
- **Print at actual size.** In the browser's print window, keep the scale at 100% (actual size). "Fit to page" shifts the print off the labels.
- Press `?` to see every **keyboard shortcut**.
- Questions and bug reports go to [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Isaac-Kwon/LabelLoom/issues).
