## v0.1.2

### New

- **About dialog** (File › 프로그램 정보): shows the version and build, author details, the AI-use disclosure, and the full license text for LabelLoom and every bundled library.
- LabelLoom is now licensed under **LGPL-3.0-or-later**. Third-party licenses are also written to `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt`.

### Fixed

- Crop marks now line up with the bleed edge.
- Dragging past the edge of the canvas no longer selects text in the surrounding panels.
- On the full-screen quick print page, Esc now takes you back to the editor dialog it was opened from.
