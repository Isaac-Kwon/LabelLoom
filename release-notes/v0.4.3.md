## v0.4.3

### Improved

- The 데이터 가져오기 window now explains pasting from the clipboard: the browser may ask for permission when you press **클립보드에서 붙여넣기**. If you did not allow it, or opened LabelLoom as a file, press Ctrl+V (⌘V on a Mac) instead. Pasted content is read only in your browser and is never sent anywhere.
