## v0.4.2

### New

- **Paste a list.** In the 데이터 가져오기 window, press Ctrl+V (⌘V on a Mac) or **클립보드에서 붙여넣기** to import a range copied from Excel or Google Sheets, or tab- or comma-separated text copied from another site. Columns are matched the same way as for a file. When you paste an updated list with **뒤에 추가**, rows whose **키 열** (ID by default) is already in the table get the new values and keep their print check and print history, and only new rows are added at the end.

### Fixed

- Tab-separated text files and URLs whose cells contain many commas are no longer split at the commas.
